Father's Rights & Statutory Protections (PA Law)

23 Pa.C.S. § 5328(b) — Gender Neutrality:
Pennsylvania law strictly prohibits court preference based on parent gender. Fathers have equal statutory rights to claim sole or shared legal and physical custody.
Pa.R.C.P. 1910.16-4 — Shared Custody Support Offset:
Fathers exercising 40% or more annual overnight custody (146+ nights) receive an automatic reduction in basic child support calculations.
